Funding Without SoFi

SoFi is already half gone and there is no replacement vendor to buy. The fundings have to come from inside the funnel: the approvals already won, the leads already paid for, and the reps already hired.

Every figure is a monthly number from Salesforce for January–August 2026 unless it says otherwise. “Non-SoFi” means every new-business lead source except SoFi. Estimates are ranges, not promises; the first 60 days will show which levers are real.

Buying more Lending Tree, businessloans.com and Bluevine leads kept the deal count flat in July–August. It cannot hold revenue, because those deals are $45K instead of $71K. So the plan targets two numbers: non-SoFi fundings, and revenue per non-SoFi deal.

Where the fundings come from

Five levers, ranked by what they can add each month

Target: +200 to +300 non-SoFi fundings per month by day 90. That covers SoFi at its July–August level and most of its first-half level. None of it needs a new lead vendor.

Close the approvals you already have Biggest lever

Non-SoFi close-on-approvals is 24% today and was 28% in 2024. Each point on ~2,200 non-SoFi approvals a month is 22 fundings. About 700 non-SoFi deals a month die after a $25K+ approval for reachable reasons; the median approval is $65K.

  • 24-hour rule: every pre-approval is presented to the merchant within one business day. Managers get an “approved, not presented” queue.
  • Any approval untouched for 48 hours auto-reassigns to a senior closer.
  • Seven-day expiring-approval text and email cadence. Lost-contact deals die at day 8 on average.
  • Second-offer rule: no “did not like the approval” above $50K closes until it has been shopped to one more lender.

Re-work the $300K+ leads that were assigned and never finished Already paid for

These are SoFi-sized businesses that asked for money this year. They were handed to a rep and stalled. Reps get ~230 leads a month and move on; nobody circles back.

  • Never reached: ~5,900 $300K+ leads a month end in “Attempting to Contact.” Put every ATC lead older than 7 days into an automated multi-channel cadence (text, email, auto-dial) that books the rep a callback. Aim to convert 5% into opps.
  • Stalled on documents: ~1,150 a month are Qualified and ~500 are Incomplete Package but never became an opp. Give them a doc-chase workflow with a bank-link option and a dedicated ops chaser instead of the rep. Aim to convert 15%.

Turn lost approvals into a channel 1% today

Win-backs are 1% of fundings. January–August left about 5,700 non-SoFi deals with $25K+ approvals lost for reachable reasons, and 2025 left a similar pile. The Deal Lost distribution queue converted 31–40% of leads into opps in the years it was used, then went quiet.

  • Automated 30, 60 and 90-day re-approach into the Deal Lost queue, starting with 2026 losses over $50K.
  • Route re-engaged deals to a small closer pod, not back to the rep who lost them.
  • Reasons to lead with: holding off on funds, too expensive, lost to competitor. Skip declined-starter and contract-issue losses.

Ramp the new-hire teams, pause AE hiring Capacity

Leads per rep fell to 196 in August and SoFi’s decline pushes it lower; more reps now means thinner slices. The three new-hire teams (Kim, Kanner, Newberry) fund 7–8 deals per rep-month against 9–10 for the veteran teams, on about 19 reps.

  • Freeze AE hiring until non-SoFi leads per rep are back above 230.
  • Scripted offer presentation and a 30-day shadow-closer program for reps under 6 months; first-quarter reps close 14% of submissions vs 17% for veterans.
  • Send the low-tier leads (sub-580 credit or under $200K revenue) to VSS and new hires. They are 44% of non-SoFi opps but 11.5% of non-SoFi revenue. Veterans get those hours back for approvals and Tiers 4–6.

Route the SoFi-profile deals to the lenders that actually fund Speed and margin

Each deal now goes to 12 lenders. On Deck funds 19% of deals from 5.5% of submissions, about 21 submissions per funding. Twelve lenders receive 35% of submissions and return 13% of fundings at 170 to 1,000 submissions each. Fewer, better-matched submissions mean faster offers and lenders who answer the phone.

  • 650+ credit and $700K+ revenue deals go first to On Deck, Fundation, Idea Financial and Peac, which fund 13–20% of what they pre-approve.
  • Cap blanket submissions at 6 per deal; the rest by profile rule.
  • Protect deal size: the $100–250K approval band closes 35% and pays the most; push term loan and line-of-credit products for prime-ish deals so revenue per deal does not drift from $4.6K toward $3.9K.

One pool that might be the biggest of all

About 1,900 SoFi leads and 1,300 other $300K+ leads a month were created in 2026 with a workable status and no Distribution Engine first-assignment stamp on the lead. Some are existing customers routed to Renewals and some are duplicates, and the stamp is missing on partner-queue leads, so this is unverified. A one-hour check: list 2026 leads with no distribution log at all, by source and status. If even a third are truly unworked, that is 1,000+ SoFi-sized businesses a month nobody called, and it becomes lever zero.
